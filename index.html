WHO WE ARE

About Congolese Diaspora in Kenya

United by our identity, strengthened by solidarity and committed to a better future.

UNITY • SOLIDARITY • DEVELOPMENT

A Community of Opportunities

The Congolese Diaspora in Kenya (DCK) is a registered organization predominantly composed of refugees, entrepreneurs, skilled professionals, students, pastors and humanitarians from the Democratic Republic of Congo.

Our community is known for its resilience, adaptability and strong transnational networks.

DCK aims to become a professional, entrepreneurial and development-oriented organization supporting its members while building bridges between the Democratic Republic of Congo, Kenya and East Africa.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Vision & Mission

◎

Our Vision

To build an aspiring, empowered and prosperous Congolese community in Kenya, where every member can live with dignity, opportunity and belonging.

We envision a community that is united in purpose, resilient in the face of challenges and capable of contributing meaningfully to both Kenyan society and the wider Congolese diaspora.

✦

Our Mission

To empower Congolese in Kenya through education, skills development, economic empowerment and community support that strengthens social inclusion.

We work to connect people to opportunities, promote cooperation and build practical solutions that improve livelihoods, encourage leadership and foster long-term unity among members.

DCK exists to create a strong network of solidarity, where members grow together, support one another and turn shared challenges into lasting opportunities for progress and hope.

WHAT WE DO

Our Main Objectives

Creating opportunities and building a stronger and more inclusive community.

🎓

Enhance Education & Skills Development

Improving access to education, vocational training, language proficiency and professional skills development for congolese, to enable them achieve greater employability and self-reliance;

💼

Promote Economic Empowerment and Sustainable Livelihoods

Strengthening the economic well-being of the congolese community by Supporting entrepreneurship, financial literacy,access to savings,and credit opportunities, and income-generating initiatives.

🤝

Strengthen Community Support & Social Inclusion

Fostering a united,resilient, and inclusive congolese community by providing social support, promoting cultural integration, advocating for members' rights and welfare, and encouraging active community participation and partnerships.
